# Architecture

Source-specific acquisition is proposed to produce immutable, source-qualified captures. Normalization and analytical projections may consume those captures; neither the query engine nor an external vocabulary admits evidence or establishes incident facts. A strategic assessment should link supporting and contradicting observations, retain alternatives and uncertainty, and be dated. Similarity in tools or techniques alone is not attribution. Replay must respect the availability of the specific source revision and assessment at the cutoff.

The registry distinguishes directly queryable datasets from proposed import routes. `requires-ingestion` selects a source-side route for this project; it does not assert that third-party BigQuery mirrors do not exist. `indirect` means the named material is accessed through another source, not necessarily that a standalone table exists. A BigQuery entry is a discovery lead, not permission to query or evidence that an adapter is implemented.

Future BigQuery adapters must be read-only, use partition filters where applicable, set a maximum bytes billed and bounded query scope, and capture immutable results and provenance as specified in [bigquery.cue](upstream/bigquery.cue). Verify table existence, schema, partitioning, permissions, location and cost before execution. OpenAlex's third-party snapshot may be unpartitioned; a partition filter cannot be assumed for every table. GDG records changes in published news content and can help identify deltas, but a changed article is not itself a verified incident change.
