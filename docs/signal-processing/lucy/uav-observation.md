# Lucy | Route-aware UAV observation

**Draft scope increase · Montréal, Québec · 21 November 2026**
**Baseline:** Lucy Secure Courier MVP v1.0. **Proposed status:** optional field trial. No aircraft, site, flight permission or field result has been confirmed.

## Capability contract

Dispatch may view a live, silent image of the **outdoor approach and handoff** while a designated on-site pilot supervises the aircraft in visual line of sight (VLOS). Clara opts in to carrying a paired beacon. The recipient agrees to the observation before the session starts. The aircraft can receive a route in advance. Only the segment needed for the observation goes to the aircraft. The pilot approves its flight path, area, height limits, operating window and recovery location.

**Example.** Dispatch knows the rideshare’s intended road route. The pilot loads an aircraft plan for the final outdoor approach at the staffed handoff site. The UAV flies that plan or holds an approved viewpoint. Where supported, the camera uses Clara’s beacon position as a cue. If the car takes another street or Clara enters the building, the UAV stays within its flight limits and dispatch sees that coverage ended. Expanding coverage to the full rideshare trip would require a new operating assessment and, where applicable, special authorization.

| Input or output | Contract behavior |
|---|---|
| Courier itinerary | Gives dispatch the expected approach and timing. A road-navigation update cannot rewrite the active flight plan. |
| Aircraft plan | Carries a versioned set of waypoints or an observation point, plus a separately defined flight boundary, altitude limits and recovery plan. A waypoint list alone cannot enforce containment. The pilot verifies the loaded version before launch. |
| Courier beacon | Supplies a time-stamped position for the consenting courier during the session. It may cue framing inside the approved area. A stale position, lost pairing, or departure from the observation area ends automatic framing. Reacquisition requires a fresh valid signal for the same session. |
| Pilot and remote viewer | The pilot can interrupt automated flight and recover locally. Dispatch has authenticated, view-only access, with an explicit live, interrupted or ended status and last-frame time. The remote screen does not satisfy the pilot’s VLOS requirement. |
| Custody system | Only the verified recipient’s authenticated acceptance, linked to the package and seal, produces the custody receipt. Video, beacon proximity, loss of video and arrival each have zero authority to change custody. The normal delivery can proceed if observation is unavailable. |

**Implementation boundary.** Model the observation session separately from the existing CUE shipment contract. Suggested fields are `sessionID`, `siteID`, `aircraftPlanRevision`, `aircraftProfile`, `pilotID`, `authorizationRef` where applicable, `observationStatus` and `lastFrameAt`. Keep the dispatch viewer read-only and the custody API independent. If implementing the proposal, project validated CUE types to JSON Schema and application types. Keep geographic containment, telemetry freshness, access expiry and pilot override as runtime checks.

## Canadian requirements for the proposed flight

**Transport Canada issues pilot certificates and registers aircraft. NAV CANADA authorizes eligible flights in airspace it controls.** A NAV Drone map check or a saved plan is different from receiving a required authorization. Determine the category from the actual flight and the aircraft’s **as-flown weight**, including its battery, guards, lights and attachments. A beacon carried by Clara is outside that weight.

| Operating profile | Requirements and application to Lucy |
|---|---|
| **Micro, under 250 g** | Generally no drone registration or pilot certificate. Transport Canada recommends remaining in sight and below 400 ft. Reckless or negligent operation, emergency-site restrictions, and restricted airspace rules still apply. Flying at an advertised outdoor event requires a Special Flight Operations Certificate (SFOC), including for micro aircraft. Use the Micro category in NAV Drone and check site rules and time-specific restrictions. Lucy adopts on-site VLOS for the trial. |
| **Small, 250 g through 25 kg, Basic** | At **exactly 250 g**, register and mark the aircraft and carry the relevant pilot certificate and registration proof. A Basic certificate involves an online exam. Plan a VLOS flight in uncontrolled airspace with more than 30 m horizontal clearance from uninvolved people, more than 5.6 km from a certified airport and more than 1.9 km from a heliport, following Transport Canada’s Basic guidance. A qualifying Basic flight does not require a NAV CANADA controlled-airspace authorization. |
| **Small, 250 g through 25 kg, Advanced** | Where the flight needs controlled airspace or closer proximity to people, use an Advanced-qualified pilot, the aircraft safety declaration applicable to the operation, and the required permissions. Advanced qualification includes an exam and flight review. For NAV CANADA-controlled airspace, obtain authorization through NAV Drone before launch and fly within its limits. Other controlling authorities can apply. A declaration for controlled airspace alone does not qualify the aircraft for flight over people. |
| **Future full-route or beyond visual line of sight (BVLOS)** | For a small enterprise aircraft, the Level 1 Complex lower-risk BVLOS pathway requires a qualified pilot, an RPAS Operator Certificate, eligible equipment and specified low-population conditions in uncontrolled airspace. A continuous BVLOS flight over densely populated Montréal falls outside that pathway and requires Transport Canada special authorization. Controlled-airspace coordination is a separate requirement where applicable. A smaller aircraft, a beacon or a working video link does not establish that a particular citywide flight is safe or permitted. |

For either trial configuration, verify actual weight on the day, airspace and notices, nearby aerodromes and heliports, site access, weather, people nearby and landing options. A battery or payload change that crosses 250 g triggers reassessment **before** launch. No Montréal site has been selected. ## Equipment and privacy

HOVERAir documents beacon-assisted following on the X1 PRO/PROMAX. DJI documents waypoint planning and remote viewing in FlightHub 2. These sources verify individual capabilities. **A single candidate aircraft and firmware supporting route execution, beacon-cued camera control, flight boundaries and private livestreaming simultaneously remains unverified.** Check the entire combination before purchase or flight. If the hardware offers mutually exclusive modes, use a preplanned fixed view with pilot framing for the handoff trial and mark beacon-cued route operation unsupported. [HOVERAir beacon guide](https://hoverair.com/blogs/guide/hoverlink) · [DJI FlightHub 2](https://enterprise.dji.com/flighthub-2)

Use a dummy shipment and volunteers. Complete a privacy impact assessment for the proposed service and document why live video is necessary for the stated purpose. Provide a normal delivery option if either participant declines. For the trial, limit access to the designated dispatcher, disable audio and recording, verify device and cloud storage behavior, and expire viewing access at session end. Keep bystanders and private windows outside the intended frame. Stop viewing if consent is withdrawn. Retain only test outcomes, timings and access events for a documented review period. [Québec private-sector privacy law](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/pdf/cs/P-39.1.pdf) · [Québec consent and necessity guidance](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/information-entreprises-privees/consentement-personnes-entreprises)

## Field test and decision

Start on the bench or in simulation. Rehearse loss of aircraft control link, unsafe position estimates and route deviations there. Fly only at a selected, permitted site after the pilot has verified the failsafe behavior for that aircraft and assessed even the return or landing path. Use consenting participants, an on-site pilot, and a separate remote viewer. A staged handoff on foot is sufficient to test the observation window.

| Test | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| Weight and site classification | Dry-run 249 g and 250 g records. The latter requests registration and pilot qualification. A missing required site or airspace authorization blocks the proposed flight. Check the real aircraft’s measured weight separately. |
| Route and beacon together | On the exact hardware and firmware, verify that the loaded plan revision matches the approved one and framing can respond to the paired beacon while the aircraft remains within the approved volume. If either mode cancels the other, record the limitation. |
| Courier deviates, goes indoors or withdraws | The aircraft stays in its approved volume. The viewer loses the live observation of Clara when she leaves view, and withdrawal ends viewing. The pilot can end the flight independently. |
| Lost beacon, video or control link | Test each separately. Beacon loss ends automatic framing. A disconnected viewer sees an interrupted status and frame age. Control-link loss invokes the pilot-verified recovery behavior. An unauthorized viewer receives no feed. |
| Staged handoff and custody | In three proposed outdoor repetitions, record whether dispatch saw the exchange, stream interruptions, longest gap, usable viewing time and landing battery. Repeat once with observation disabled. An authenticated recipient receipt governs every successful closure. |
| Release decision | Require zero observed boundary breaches, unauthorized views, retained trial footage or custody transitions caused by observation. Report visible handoffs as a fraction of attempts and list interruptions. Three trials are a feasibility check. They cannot establish a citywide reliability rate. |

**Decision requested after testing:** qualify the exact configuration for a defined handoff site, keep only the fixed-view version, or discontinue aerial observation. No flight testing or regulatory applications are part of this draft.
