# Gym unit

Canonical authority lives under `contracts/personal/gym/`.

This unit records training exposure, mechanical observations, recovery observations, measurement evidence, and later derived adaptation projections. Acquisition may be conversational, video-derived, scale/device-derived, or imported, but observations must remain distinct from interpretation.

Do not infer diagnoses, causal mechanisms, weakness, correction, or readiness from raw statements during capture. Preserve uncertainty and missing observations explicitly. Corrections append/supersede prior observations rather than silently rewriting them.
