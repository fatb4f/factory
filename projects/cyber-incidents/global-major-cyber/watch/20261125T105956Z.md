### Drupal core exploitation reported on live sites

`drupal-cve-2026-132165` · Maintainer-reported exploitation • Drupal risk 20/25 · Global advisory; affected sites unidentified

Drupal dated the earliest exploitation reported to its security team. Its advisory, the KEV entry and Cyber Centre update refer to the same CVE.

- Drupal published its advisory on November 18. Its updated account dates the earliest reported live-site exploitation to November 19 at 19:00 UTC. [SA-CORE-2026-017](https://www.drupal.org/sa-core-2026-017)
- Drupal rates the issue 20/25. Under vulnerable conditions a site takeover or code execution is possible. The fixed releases for 10.6, 11.3 and 11.4 are 10.6.22, 11.3.22 and 11.4.13 respectively. [SA-CORE-2026-017](https://www.drupal.org/sa-core-2026-017)
- CISA added the CVE on November 24. Catalog revision 2026.11.24 contains 1,795 entries. The Canadian Cyber Centre names the same CVE in Update 1. [CVE-2026-132165](https://github.com/cisagov/kev-data/blob/02cf8df0c1e2a06cc12842214d3c5e9b0ece97d9/known_exploited_vulnerabilities.json) · [AV26-1186](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/alerts-advisories/drupal-security-advisory-av26-1186)
