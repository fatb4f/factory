# Intelligence ontologies

ATT&CK and DISARM offer versioned vocabularies for behaviors; MISP galaxies and taxonomies provide structured reference material. CCO / IAO-Intel, PROV-O and SKOS are candidates for expressing concepts, provenance and vocabularies. STIX and Attack Flow can represent exchange or relationships; CACAO describes playbooks. These standards are not interchangeable. Preserve source identifiers, versions and mapping losses, and evaluate each license before use. A vocabulary mapping does not prove a behavior occurred.

References: [ATT&CK data](https://attack.mitre.org/resources/attack-data-and-tools/), [MISP Galaxy](https://github.com/MISP/misp-galaxy), [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/), [SKOS](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/), [CACAO](https://docs.oasis-open.org/cacao/security-playbooks/v2.0/cs01/security-playbooks-v2.0-cs01.html).
