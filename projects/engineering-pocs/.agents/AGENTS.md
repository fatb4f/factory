# engineering-pocs execution procedure

Semantic authority is `contracts/projects/engineering-pocs/`.

This project owns Factory decisions about what to prototype or test. World domains observe reality; this project decides what Factory should build.

Current execution is `contract-only`; the registry task is disabled.

A future POC qualification must begin from an admitted engineering path plus an explicit industrial problem. Initiative demand and resource-allocation candidates may raise priority, but neither may substitute for the engineering mechanism or industrial problem evidence. `poc-candidate` dispositions from engineering watch are hypotheses only.

A POC becomes `prototype-ready` only when its proposition, bounded test, expected learning, and falsifiers are explicit and material source-domain coverage gaps do not invalidate the experiment rationale.
