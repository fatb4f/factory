# Recovery checkpoint

Recovery is part of the same training run. Prefer checkpoints around T+12, T+24, T+48, and T+72 when useful, but do not fabricate missing checkpoints.

Capture the smallest useful vector:

- elapsed hours or timestamp;
- DOMS, 0-5 by named region;
- energy available, 0-4;
- cognitive availability, 0-4;
- task initiation state;
- sleepiness state;
- subjective recovery state;
- gait and key movement availability;
- linked measurements/media when relevant.

The checkpoint records state only. Recovery cost, duration, and readiness are downstream projections.
