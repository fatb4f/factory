# Attribution frameworks

Separate observation, public claim, and analytical hypothesis. Record who made an attribution, when, on what cited evidence, and with what caveats. Compare competing explanations against supporting and contradicting evidence, dependence among sources, collection gaps and plausible alternatives. Do not infer actor, sponsor or intent solely from shared infrastructure, code or techniques. Preserve unknown and disputed states and reassess when source revisions change. Evaluate calibration, false attribution, abstention and temporal leakage before operational use.
