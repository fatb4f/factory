# INF1120 — Directives pour les étudiants aux examens

> Text projection of `directivesPourEtudiants.pdf`. Canonical source identity: `sha256:3ea05fa0716d2764267377feb0e553fb163f4e69fa3a23498d9076d47a7aa93a`.

- Exam rooms are published on the Moodle course site under **ÉVALUATIONS**.
- Student card is **mandatory** and is checked.
- Write your name and permanent code on the exam copy.
- A signature is required on the supplied name list when submitting the exam.
- No document is permitted except **one 8½ × 11 sheet of notes, double-sided**.
- Electronic equipment is strictly forbidden, including computers, tablets, cellular phones, music players and calculators.
- Leaving the exam room requires submitting the exam; the student cannot then return to the room.
- Exam duration is **3 hours**.
- Invigilators do not answer questions during the exam.
