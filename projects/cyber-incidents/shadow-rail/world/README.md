# Shadow Rail World (SRW)

The versioned [ontology](ontology.cue) is the canonical vocabulary for this bounded analytical domain. Its concepts and relation descriptions provide meaning for later contracts; they are not instance schemas, admission rules, evidence, or an implemented graph. In particular, an observation, a source's claim, an evidential assessment, a hypothesis, and an attribution are distinct. Relations describe a possible analytical interpretation and do not establish an event or responsibility. Candidate actors and activities can remain unknown or disputed. A model-assisted worker may propose an assessment; it does not acquire attribution authority.

The [crosswalk](crosswalk.cue) records a curated comparison to the [CCO 2.2 merged ontology](https://github.com/CommonCoreOntology/CommonCoreOntologies/blob/v2.2/src/cco-merged/CommonCoreOntologiesMerged.ttl). The referenced labels and IRIs were checked against that release. `related-reference` signals a useful but non-equivalent comparison; it does not assert subsumption. `broader-reference` is reserved for a separately justified broader reference. `unmapped` deliberately makes no alignment. Mapping rationale records material differences; no OWL import, runtime dependency, inference, source conversion, or automatic semantic admission is implied. Future adapters must preserve version and mapping loss and justify their own mappings.

Run `bash projects/cyber-incidents/shadow-rail/world/validate.sh` for the concrete vocabulary and crosswalk and negative boundary cases. Later work must define instance identity, provenance, time, evidence admission, and typed relation endpoints before materializing a graph or projecting exchange formats. Existing or future cyber-incidents contracts remain authoritative for their own incident semantics; no integration is asserted here.
