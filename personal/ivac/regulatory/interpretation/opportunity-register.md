## ivac-interpretation-analogy-equivalence

Sources: P-9.2.1, r.1, art.25 al.2 [gazette-2021-39:451248–451301]; P-9.2.1, r.1, art.24 al.2 [gazette-2021-39:450348–450394]

**Issue.** Unlisted presentation and comparator scope

**Claimant reading.** An unlisted permanent presentation must be evaluated against an analogous described situation of equivalent daily-life consequences, instead of rejected merely because its diagnosis is absent.

**Restrictive reading.** The comparator must preserve equivalent severity, relevant unit function, objective clinical support and specific exclusions; analogy cannot bypass an express rule or create an unlisted free-standing unit.

**Legal support.** Express provisions anchored in legalSource; this is a candidate analysis, not a case-specific legal opinion.

**Counterauthority and limits.** The comparator must preserve equivalent severity, relevant unit function, objective clinical support and specific exclusions; analogy cannot bypass an express rule or create an unlisted free-standing unit. Express unit-specific exclusions and professional proof remain controlling; no contrary cases were invented. J.V. c. SAAQ,2012 QCTAQ 04180,paras67–95: analogy rejected where an appropriate psychiatric unit applied and the proposed consciousness comparator lacked medical support. SAAQ regime; persuasive only.

**Clinical prerequisites.** Qualified clinician explains matched and differing functional consequences, permanence and attribution.

**Evidence required.** Comparator matrix, clinical exam, longitudinal function and reasoned alternative comparator.

**Coverage gaps.** Comparator boundaries and burden in post-2021 IVAC jurisprudence remain incompletely researched. Independent legal review and complete class-path audit pending.

**Potential impact.** classification

**Status.** Legal confidence: supported; clinical confidence: not-assessed; review: candidate; adjudication: not-determined.
