# Session close

Capture the immediate post-session state without predicting recovery.

Preferred fields when available:

- energy available, 0-4;
- cognitive availability, 0-4;
- gait state;
- hinge/knee-flexion availability if meaningfully changed;
- named local responses (`none`, `loaded`, `fatigued`, `discomfort`, `unknown`);
- notable direct events such as cramp or pain occurrence.

Do not mark the session as successful, excessive, or ready to progress here. The session outcome remains provisional until recovery evidence is available.
