## Canadian surveillance and privacy

### Senate committee reports Bill C-22 with amendments

`ca-45-1-c-22` · Committee report presented with amendments · Canada, federal

The November 26 Ninth Report provides the amendment text sought in the previous run. LEGISinfo now records Senate report stage.

- The Senate committee presented its Ninth Report on November 26. Parliament records completion of committee consideration and the start of Senate report stage. [C-22 (45-1)](https://www.parl.ca/legisinfo/en/bill/45-1/c-22) · [Senate Journals 107](https://sencanada.ca/en/content/sen/chamber/451/journals/107jr_2026-11-26-e)
- For clause 41 the committee proposed a necessity and proportionality test for each obligation imposed by regulation or ministerial order. It also proposed clarifying the systemic-vulnerability definition to cover risks of unauthorized alteration and specified weakening of electronic protections. The Commons-passed version required consideration of listed factors. [Senate Journals 107](https://sencanada.ca/en/content/sen/chamber/451/journals/107jr_2026-11-26-e) · [C-22 Commons third-reading text](https://www.parl.ca/documentviewer/en/45-1/bill/C-22/third-reading)

Open questions

- The report alone establishes neither enactment nor a legal obligation on a particular provider in Montréal.

Next check — Watch Senate concurrence, further amendments, third reading and any Commons response. Check the final enacted text and commencement separately.
