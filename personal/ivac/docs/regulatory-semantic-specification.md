# IVAC permanent-sequela semantics — adversarial research checkpoint

This specification belongs to dependent investigation #226, consuming Milestone 2
#222 / draft PR #223. It is a **partial candidate analysis**, not an admitted legal
interpretation or a case assessment. No personal medical records were accessed.
The original 2021 Gazette/Act are pinned and byte-verified. The October 9 bulk XML
is also verified but is not assumed to be the law applicable to any particular
benefit. Exhaustive class/path transcription and independent legal review remain
open. The accompanying register has 25 candidate questions and 25 synthetic review
scenarios; eight have executable Boolean comparison programs.

## 1. Authority, versions and proof obligations

The enabling Act P-9.2.1, particularly articles 36–41, supplies eligibility,
assessment/establishment and aggravation authority. Regulation P-9.2.1,r.1
Chapter III supplies the permanent/temporary/death routes, evaluation rules,
classification and percentage mechanics. Annex I supplies unit definitions,
measurement procedures, comparators, exclusions and aesthetic tables. A policy
cannot amend these texts. A clinical assessment establishes professionally
supported facts; an administrator or tribunal applies the legally selected rule.
An analytical table establishes neither.

The source ledger is `../regulatory/bulk-observations-20261017.json`. The register
anchors each opportunity to exact original 2021 UTF-8 text offsets and the
original artifact digest, using the retained acquisition records. An anchor's
`sourceVersion` is a representation identity, not proof of legal applicability.
Publication, publisher consolidation date, provision commencement, retrieval time,
XML member digest and case-specific legal selection must remain separate.
October 9 in a ZIP name is not a commencement date. XML `date-eev` is retained as a
publisher attribute until interpreted against the actual provision/history.

Articles179–184 require a benefit-specific transitional analysis: prior
admissibility, definitive decisions, whether incapacity/need existed, pending
applications, benefit cessation and recurrence can matter. Neither an old offence
nor a recent decision alone selects the regime. Article183 and other historical
time limits cannot be turned into present advice without an amendment audit.
The January 2026 Gazette proposal is not enactment evidence. Former I-6 decisions,
SAAQ A-25 cases and the new IVAC regime are not interchangeable.

## 2. Evaluation dependency structure

The operational dependency order is:

1. Identify the legally applicable instrument/provision versions and benefit.
2. Establish statutory qualification and relevant prejudice under the Act.
3. Obtain the prescribed/professionally competent health evaluation (reg 19–20).
4. Establish permanence (reg 23; Act 39) and clinically attributed consequences.
5. Identify leaf units and apply routing, inclusion and exclusion rules.
6. Apply unit-specific measurements and evaluate described or analogous situations.
7. Retain one highest-impact supported class per affected unit (reg 25).
8. Determine bilateral allowance and prior functional-state deductions where applicable.
9. Aggregate the eligible percentages with the article 26 staged residual procedure.
10. Separately establish the monetary base/date, prior payments, other-source offsets,
    procedural decision and any review rights. This checkpoint does not calculate money.

A later step cannot prove an earlier gate. In particular, a valid equation cannot
prove causation, a high clinical score cannot select a legal regime, and an
evidence link cannot manufacture clinical support.

Article23 defines permanence by the absence of medically foreseeable notable
improvement **or deterioration** in the short/medium term. Under that negative
scope, the evaluator must consider both directions; it is not an OR of convenient
eligibility branches. Act 39 also addresses no significant improvement and
unit-by-unit establishment. These should be reconciled, not reduced to a fixed
waiting period. Article24 excludes speculative long-term aggravation from the
present evaluation but preserves a new evaluation for actual increased prejudice;
Act 41 governs confirmed aggravation. Stability today does not mean lifelong
absence of variation.

Article24 requires recognized medical explanation supported by objective clinical
examination data. This is not a universal imaging requirement. Conversely,
patient narrative, diagnosis, elapsed time and a prescription cannot automatically
satisfy the clinical gate. Units may require particular tests, optimal therapy,
consistency or measurement methods. Reg20 accepts a diagnostic impression or
prejudice description as part of the evaluation, but that alone does not prove
permanent qualifying consequences.

plus heading 25 containing8 aesthetic units. The official Directive 2.7 describes
24 functional units; that shorthand must not collapse the annex's child units.
`interpretation/rubric-coverage.json` anchors all25 rubric spans and every leaf
identity. Each rubric's class/path review remains explicitly pending.

Rules can **route** an effect to another unit, **include** it in the current unit,
**permit additional assessment**, or **exclude** a second rating. Those are
four different operations. One diagnosis may produce distinct separately
assessable consequences. Multiple diagnoses may produce one unit's consequences.
Neither diagnosis counting nor a universal one-injury/one-unit rule follows.

Examples anchored in the register:

- Unit 14 rule 3 explicitly directs assessment of associated hand cutaneous loss
  under the upper-limb sensitivity unit. Separate sensory and dexterity findings
  still require attribution and nonduplication.
- Unit 17 rule 3 sends digestive/respiratory effects to the appropriate functions;
  it does not include every effect of a rib/abdominal injury in protection alone.
- Unit 2 includes effects such as incontinence during a seizure. Unit 8 includes
  locomotion affected by balance. Relabeling those effects is not a second unit.
- Unit 24 bundles consequences of the defined syndromes, but its rule 5 routes
  other specified cord/radicular presentations to appropriate separate units.
  Do not extend its bundling to every neurological condition.
- Aesthetic dynamic effects/device appearance and specified unit 24 effects are
  already included. A genuinely static alteration can remain assessable subject
  to the aesthetic rules. Clothing/hair coverage is not itself exclusion.

No blanket claim that every psychological, sensory or aesthetic consequence is
separately payable is supportable. Mixed causes and overlapping scopes require a
reasoned effect-to-unit allocation.

## 4. Class selection, alternatives and analogy

For a unit, let E be professionally supported evaluation findings. Each described
situation has scoped prerequisites, measurements and consequences; it may contain
OR branches, AND conditions, examples and a shared tail. Evaluate that structure
before choosing a class. Article25 then selects the supported situation of greatest
impact, with one class and one grant of its percentage per unit. It does not add
class percentages, average symptoms, assign percentages per diagnosis, or authorize
using an unrepresentative transient worst episode as the permanent state.

Article25 paragraph 2 supplies an express analogy route when a situation is not
described. Compare **equivalent gravity of daily-life consequences** with a
specified described comparator. Record matched consequences, meaningful differences,
unit scope, permanence, restrictions, objective support and contrary comparators.
This is not permission to invent a percentage, interpolate a class freely, ignore
an exclusion or treat a different statutory regime as the same schedule. The text
supports considering analogy; the precise evidentiary burden and acceptable
comparator boundaries in new-regime IVAC decisions remain research gaps.

Never mechanically split the word `ou`. Alternatives within an example, a list of
measurements, a negative condition or a description of an already combined unit
are not necessarily independent legal paths. Illustrative wording such as `telle`,
`par exemple`, `peuvent` and `pouvant` likewise needs grammatical and rule context.
A finite nested Boolean structure can be converted to disjunctive normal form,
but duplicating shared conditions can lose their scope, source identity and review
meaning. Neither extraction token counts nor formula satisfiability settle that
legal decomposition.

## 5. Unit 1: psychiatric/cognitive function

The rule 2 evaluation is global: autonomy/social efficiency in light of compensatory
strategies, technical aids and supervision/assistance; cognitive impacts on habits
of life; and affective/mental impacts evaluated with the adapted incorporated EGF.
Observed supported performance must be distinguished from unassisted capacity.
Living alone does not establish absence of assistance needs; use of strategies does
not itself prove severity. A diagnosis, a momentary clinic presentation and a
single unreasoned EGF number are insufficient substitutes for the full evaluation.

|Class|Percentage|Described routes and important boundary|
|---|---:|---|
|Below threshold|Not an assigned zero in the model|Symptoms without significant personal/social repercussions; lesser consequences|
|1|2%|Affective/mental EGF71–80 **or** regular permanent necessary prescribed medication|
|2|5%|Affective/mental EGF61–70 **or** minor cognitive deficit requiring slight adaptation|
|3|15%|Affective/mental EGF51–60 **or** light cognitive deficit affecting complex tasks; substantial adaptation language must retain its scope|
|4|35%|Affective/mental EGF41–50 **or** moderate cognitive deficit affecting usual tasks; reorganization/human-intervention paragraph has a disputed shared-tail scope|
|5|70%|Major psychiatric disorganization/reality impairment **or** severe cognitive inability with limited safe time alone; not an automatic EGF band conversion|
|6|100%|Entire/almost entire human dependence for most everyday activities; possible protective measures are not automatically mandatory institutionalization|

Class 3's cognitive branch says adaptation may justify another person's intervention;
that does not expressly require assistance actually purchased or received. Class 4
uses stronger necessity language. Whether the final assistance paragraph qualifies
both psychiatric and cognitive branches or the cognitive branch alone is retained
as a genuine candidate scope dispute, not silently resolved in code. Even the
favorable reading requires a valid EGF assessment and the broader clinical gates.

The incorporated EGF itself contains symptom-versus-function alternatives. Do not
transform those examples into cumulative symptom lists. Conversely, a lower score
outside the expressly mapped71–41 bands does not automatically imply70% or100%.
Compare the actual class 5/6 situations, or analyze analogy with explicit reasons.
Medication side effects belong in the retained description; they do not create
separate percentages inside one unit by arithmetic addition.

The annex incorporates an adapted older DSM-IV-era scale (including its printed
bibliographic wording). Its age is not legal invalidity. A contemporary specialist
can explain current examination findings in terms of the incorporated criteria,
identify uncertainty, and provide supplementary clinical evidence. This checkpoint
does not assume a modern scale is interchangeable or invent a crosswalk. It also
does not collapse etiological attribution, symptom burden, everyday function,
compensation costs, assistance necessity and actual assistance received.

## 6. Exact article 26 arithmetic and unresolved boundaries

Only already selected, legally applicable, clinically qualified percentages enter
this layer. Arithmetic has no authority to choose a path, comparator or class.
`regulatoryArithmeticPolicy` in CUE declares the mechanical stages;
`ivac_regulatory_math.py` executes them with Decimal, rejecting binary floats.

### Functional baseline

For each relevant functional unit, derive a representative current percentage C
and the percentage B of the actual pre-offence state. The retained difference is
C−B. A diagnosis or previous payment alone is not B. Missing B remains unknown;
a negative difference raises an unresolved boundary rather than being silently
clamped, negated or converted to an award. Historical bilateral allowance must
also be considered where applicable.

Article26 excludes injuries/diseases occurring **after** the offence **and unrelated**
to it. Post-offence timing alone does not establish unrelatedness. A qualified
clinical attribution is needed; temporal sequence alone cannot establish relation.
Functional baseline deductions in26(1)(d) must not be blindly copied into aesthetic
26(2), whose wording instead selects the related aesthetic situation. Any aesthetic
pre-existing-state treatment needs its own legal justification.

### Bilateral upper limbs

Only movement/maintenance and manual dexterity, right and left, participate.
At least one related qualifying permanent sequela is needed; eligible pre-existing
contralateral impairments may be included, while unrelated later ones are excluded.
The actual official XML table and original Gazette show the fraction:

`raw = (left_total + right_total) / 8`

The minimum is0.5%; the cap is the sum of the two units on the less affected side.
Keep only the first decimal, then raise1–4 to5 and6–9 to the next integer.
This is not ordinary nearest-half rounding. For raw2.09875, truncation gives2.0;
rounding the original fraction straight upward to2.5 would be incorrect.

The prose's cap/rounding precedence is not fully explicit. The implementation
requires an explicit reading and keeps both traces. A synthetic cap0.7 stress
case diverges, but **no actual eligible unit 13/14 class combination reaching that
cap has been demonstrated**. It is a speculative specification issue, not a
claimed real increase. Do not promote an unreachable numerical edge case into a
legal opportunity or choose the larger trace automatically.

### Residual aggregation

For multiple percentages sort p1≥p2≥… . Set A=p1. For each later pi calculate
`ci = round_half_up((100 − accumulated_contributions) × pi / 100, 2 places)`.
Add the contributions and, if fractional, raise the final global percentage to
the next integer. Intermediate contribution rounding happens before the next
residue. Do not add raw unit percentages, round each unit globally first, or
replace the staged procedure with an unrounded product identity.

Worked checks:

- 35 and 10 →35+6.50=41.50 →42, matching Directive 2.7's numerical example.
- 15, 10, 5 →15+8.50+3.83=27.33 →28.
- 1 and 0.1 →1+0.10=1.10 →2. A nearest-integer result of1 is not the prescribed
multi-rate ceiling.
- A single 0.5 remains0.5 in the implemented literal single-rate reading because
the global-rounding paragraph is introduced for several percentages. This boundary
is explicit; the code does not infer that one fractional rate must become1.

Directive 2.7's example reaches42 correctly but explains it by referring to a
decimal above4, whereas the regulatory final stage is upward rounding for any
fractional result. Its shorthand cannot change the regulation. The directive is
policy-only, and its revision field is a placeholder; do not treat it as an
exhaustive current interpretive code.

The suite compares Decimal results with an independently written Fraction oracle
and tests100 generated residual lists plus100 generated baseline pairs. Bilateral
thresholds, cap-order divergence, unknown/negative baselines, empty input and
float rejection have explicit examples. Vision, hearing, weighted musculoskeletal
and aesthetic table calculations are **not yet completely implemented**. Their
lookup cells, boundary inclusivity, units and rounding stages need transcription
and independently worked examples before claiming the entire calculation system.
No monetary award, annual indexation or settlement deduction is executed.

## 7. Monetary and procedural boundaries

Act 38 ties monetary amounts to those in force at establishment. An original nominal
2021 base, a current website example and the incident-year base are not substitutes
for the legally applicable indexed amount/date. Act 39 supports sequela-specific
establishment when one condition has stabilized while another is still treated;
interdependence and staged-payment reconciliation still require reasons.

Act 79/89 require written reasons;88 permits completing the record during review.
Potential review issues include a disregarded material alternative, unsupported
baseline, missing relevant unit, wrong version, wrong calculation stage or a
non-explained comparator choice. None automatically proves entitlement or invalidity.
An administrator may reject evidence on adequately explained grounds.

Under the inspected Act 85, ordinary administrative review is requested within90 days
of communication, subject to its maximum-aid exception. Article87 requires actual
reasonable-cause proof for a late request. Article90's delayed-review route has
its own90-day trigger, applicant-requested delay rule and notified professional
examination/document extension. Do not replace them by a single guessed timer.
J-3 article 110 separately gives the usual60-day social-affairs TAQ filing period
following notification, and addresses failure-to-dispose review cases. The J-3
bulk member and exact span metadata are in the research log. Applicable versions,
notification facts and extensions must be checked before calculating a deadline.
Article82 reconsideration,83 changed circumstances,87 lateness relief and a TAQ
challenge are distinct mechanisms. J-3 article 154 tribunal reconsideration is not
an unrestricted second merits appeal.

## 8. Jurisprudence and administrative practice

One directly relevant new-regime adverse decision was obtained as **primary full
text**, beyond its SOQUIJ blog summary:

[C.M. c. Ministre de la Justice (IVAC),2026 QCTAQ 02260,12February2026](https://citoyens.soquij.qc.ca/php/decision.php?ID=FB95BCDDBEF93A8D5737BF2A0400EABF),
fileSAS-M-336316-2409. Its original HTML digest is in the authority register. The
TAQ rejects excluding a civil settlement from same-prejudice anti-cumulation merely
because no lawsuit was filed and confirms reconsideration on the stated facts.
The psychiatric class was expressly **not contested**: the decision cannot be
cited as deciding EGF thresholds, analogy, path scope or class 3 entitlement. It
illustrates contextual interpretation and an adverse limit on an attractive
literal argument. It is a persuasive administrative decision; appeal/finality
status was not independently checked.

A second decision was verified from **primary published full text**:

[J.V. c. Société de l’assurance automobile du Québec, 2012 QCTAQ 04180](https://citoyens.soquij.qc.ca/php/decision.php?ID=7D6DF36832DE28C2857E0B469AA5566E).
It applies the SAAQ regulation, article 5, not IVAC article 25. Paragraphs 67–95
reject a consciousness-unit analogy because the condition could be evaluated in
the psychiatric unit and the proposed comparator lacked sufficient medical
support. Paragraphs 97–113 nevertheless assign psychiatric class 4 after assessing
the full functional evidence, although reported EGF opinions were 55 and the
administrator favored class 3. Both sides matter: this is a useful candidate
argument against an unreasoned numerical EGF ceiling, and a substantial caution
against shopping for a higher analogy where an appropriate unit already applies.
It does not make unemployment an automatic 35% criterion, erase the statutory
bands, prove that every shared-tail paragraph is branch-specific, or establish a
binding new-regime IVAC rule. The text/version comparison and expert reconciliation
remain prerequisites. Finality/appeal status was not independently checked.

There is no verified new-regime IVAC analogy/class-path decision establishing all
favorable readings in this checkpoint. Higher-court interpretation principles
mentioned in C.M. are not presented as independently analyzed holdings here.

[Official IVAC Directive 2.7](https://www.ivac.qc.ca/a-propos/Documents/2-7-forfaitaire-sequelles-aggravation-atteinte-integrite.pdf)
is useful administrative practice evidence for residual aggregation, baseline,
aggravation and staged establishment. It remains below legislation. The older
IVAC policy manual concerns a potentially different regime and is not imported
as current law. The research log distinguishes original-text inspection,
search/discovery failures and unverified leads.

## 9. Factory model reconciliation and implementation status

Existing canonical CUE retains legal text, selected normalized classes and
qualification boundaries. Existing all-of criteria within any-of alternatives can
represent a **reviewed finite decomposition**. They do not resolve scope disputes,
analogy, comparability, shared-rule conflicts, conditional formulae or completeness
by themselves. Fixed/range/not-specified percentages cannot encode an entire
weighted calculation/table dependency graph. A supported criterion path does not
certify every global evaluation rule or maximum comparable class.

The demonstrated extension is therefore a **separate candidate research annotation
contract**, containing exact anchors, opposing readings, authority weight,
clinical prerequisites, evidence needs, distinct legal/clinical uncertainty and
synthetic scenarios. It cannot impersonate `#RegulatoryCompendium`, a supported
clinical finding or `#RegulatoryQualification`. It rejects invented legal-review
status, duplicate opportunity IDs, dangling scenario IDs, version mismatches,
claims of exhaustive review and automatic selection. No canonical adjudicative
or clinical semantics were weakened. The larger proposed entity catalogue has
not been automatically admitted as nine new canonical domains.

CUE owns the arithmetic policy and explicit synthetic comparison expressions.
Generated JSON Schema/Pydantic v2 scenario/comparison types are projections. Eight
Ibis comparisons lower validated `factory.analytics-ir/v1` plans through the
existing adapter into in-memory DuckDB/Arrow. One tests a genuine shared-tail
scope dispute; seven test intentionally overrestrictive transformations, labeled
as regression cases **rather than equally defensible legal alternatives**.
All 25 review scenarios keep their opposing reading and unresolved boundary;
25 complete legal evaluators are not claimed. Diagnostic input authorization in
analytics IR is not legal admission. BigQuery remains optional and untested.

Full canonical compendium/qualification generation remains subject to the CUE
exporter field-dependent-comparison gap documented in Milestone 2. Schema validity
never establishes the legal validity of a candidate reading. Remaining gates:
complete all48 unit class/path/table reviews, audit enacted versions and case-law
both ways, verify appeal status, obtain independent legal review, and qualify the
unimplemented calculation families. Issue#226 remains open for those gates.
