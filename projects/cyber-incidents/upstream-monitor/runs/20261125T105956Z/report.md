## Canadian surveillance and privacy

### C-297 would make certain personalized prices reviewable

`ca-45-1-c-297` · First reading • November 23 · Canada, federal

The official first-reading text resolves the earlier clause-access gap. The proposal turns on a profit purpose, a material price difference and inadequate disclosure.

- Clause 2 would add section 74.051 to the Competition Act. It covers consumers charged materially different prices for comparable goods or services for profit maximization without fair and adequate disclosure. Paragraphs (2)–(3) cover personal data and exclude adjustments based solely on generally applicable market factors. [C-297 (45-1)](https://www.parl.ca/documentviewer/en/45-1/bill/C-297/first-reading)
- Clause 1 would let the Competition Commissioner assist qualifying foreign investigations into this category. Existing section 74.012 requires written purpose and confidentiality commitments before disclosure, excludes foreign contraventions with penal consequences under Canadian law, and requires consideration of reciprocal assistance. [C-297 (45-1)](https://www.parl.ca/documentviewer/en/45-1/bill/C-297/first-reading) · [Competition Act, s. 74.012](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-34/section-74.012.html)
- Clause 3 adds the conduct to the subsequent-order provision. Clauses 4–7 add it to electronic-commerce enforcement, disclosure and use provisions, including information flows involving the CRTC, Competition Commissioner and Privacy Commissioner. Existing section 60 requires a written agreement or arrangement and limits use and redisclosure. [C-297 (45-1)](https://www.parl.ca/documentviewer/en/45-1/bill/C-297/first-reading) · [S.C. 2010, c. 23, s. 60](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-1.6/section-60.html)

Open questions

- Application of materiality and fair disclosure to a particular Montréal transaction would depend on the facts and any enacted wording.

Next check — Compare each subsequent bill version with clauses 1–7 and check for committee amendments, assent and commencement.
