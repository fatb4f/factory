# canada-climate-readiness execution procedure

Semantic authority is `contracts/world/canada-climate-readiness/`. The selected phase is `event-watch`.

Track Canadian and Quebec climate-adaptation and infrastructure-readiness developments when they create engineering obligations, modify design requirements, redirect infrastructure spending, expose physical constraints, or create demand for industrial capacity.

Prefer primary adaptation strategies, infrastructure programs, standards/code bodies, regulators, utilities, municipalities, project proponents and official funding/procurement records.

Record hazards and resilience requirements only as stated by admitted sources. Do not infer physical asset failure, industrial shortages, clean-energy adequacy or investment attractiveness from policy announcements.

Write admitted runs under `world/canada-climate-readiness/runs/<run-id>/manifest.json` conforming to `#RunManifest` and return one contracted watch outcome.
