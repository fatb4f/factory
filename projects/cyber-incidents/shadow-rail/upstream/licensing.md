# Licensing and acquisition

Registry entries are candidates, not grants of permission. Before acquisition or redistribution, review the specific dataset version, license, attribution, permitted use, access conditions, and any restrictions on underlying content. Record the review and source URL with the capture. A public BigQuery table can still incur query charges and does not grant rights to republish underlying works; a third-party mirror may differ from its upstream. Official records, standards and repositories have their own terms. No blanket licensing clearance is asserted here.
